# Faculty pre-launch Google Form — question draft

**Status:** Draft for product-owner approval. Do not create or distribute the
Google Form until the questions, privacy notice, owners, and retention policy are
approved.

## Recommended structure

Use two linked forms rather than one oversized form:

1. **Faculty onboarding and public profile** — one response per faculty member.
2. **Research project intake** — one response per project, repeatable by the same
   faculty member.

Collect institutional email automatically. Never request passwords, OTPs,
Aadhaar/PAN numbers, bank details, API keys, or unrelated personal information.
Verification evidence and employee references must remain restricted to the
authorized verification team and must not appear on public profiles.

## Form A — faculty onboarding and public profile

### Section 1: notice, consent, and identity

1. **Institutional email address** — collected automatically; required; private.
2. **Full name as it should appear publicly** — short answer; required.
3. **NSUT employee/faculty reference number** — short answer; required for
   verification; private.
4. **Designation** — dropdown; required: Professor, Associate Professor,
   Assistant Professor, Visiting/Adjunct Faculty, Research Staff, Other.
5. **Department and campus** — canonical department dropdown; required.
6. **Employment/association type** — dropdown; required: Regular, Contract,
   Visiting/Adjunct, Research Staff, Other.
7. **May NSUT Connect publish the approved profile fields identified as public in
   this form?** — Yes/No; required.
8. **I confirm that the submitted information is accurate and that I am authorized
   to share the listed links and material.** — checkbox; required.

### Section 2: public academic profile

9. **Short professional biography** — paragraph; required; recommended 100–500
   words.
10. **Education and qualifications** — paragraph; required; degree, institution,
    and discipline only.
11. **Primary research area** — short answer; required.
12. **Additional research keywords** — short answer; optional; comma-separated,
    up to ten.
13. **Courses or laboratories currently associated with** — paragraph; optional.
14. **Office location** — short answer; optional/public.
15. **Student meeting or office hours** — short answer; optional/public.
16. **Preferred public contact email** — short answer; optional; defaults to the
    institutional email.
17. **Google Scholar URL** — URL or `NA`; optional.
18. **ORCID identifier/URL** — short answer or `NA`; optional.
19. **Institutional/personal academic website** — URL or `NA`; optional.
20. **GitHub or research-code profile** — URL or `NA`; optional.
21. **LinkedIn profile** — URL or `NA`; optional.
22. **Public CV/resume PDF or document link** — URL or `NA`; optional. Confirm
    that link sharing is enabled for intended viewers.

### Section 3: research outputs and discovery

23. **List up to five publications or research outputs to feature at launch** —
    paragraph; title, co-authors, date, DOI/public URL; optional.
24. **Preferred publication profile for future synchronization** — multiple
    choice: ORCID, Google Scholar, institutional page, none, other; optional.
25. **Research facilities, datasets, instruments, or laboratories students may
    discover through the portal** — paragraph; optional.
26. **Topics on which students may approach you for mentorship** — paragraph;
    optional.

### Section 4: collaboration and campus content

27. **Are you open to faculty-to-faculty collaboration requests through the
    portal?** — Yes/No; required.
28. **Preferred collaboration types** — checkboxes: Methodology, Dataset,
    Equipment/Laboratory, Co-supervision, Publication, Grant proposal,
    Interdisciplinary project, Other; shown when Question 27 is Yes.
29. **Are you willing to publish departmental news or research updates?** —
    Yes/No; required.
30. **Are you willing to publish student opportunities/events?** — Yes/No;
    required.
31. **Would you like launch-orientation support?** — checkboxes: Profile setup,
    Project listing, Applicant review, Publication entry, News/opportunities,
    Accessibility support, No support needed.
32. **Preferred contact method for launch coordination** — multiple choice:
    Institutional email, Department coordinator, Scheduled meeting.
33. **Anything the implementation team should know before publishing your
    profile?** — paragraph; optional/private.

## Form B — research project intake

Submit this form once for every project the faculty member wants listed.

### Section 1: ownership and listing basics

1. **Faculty institutional email** — collected automatically; required; used to
   associate project ownership.
2. **Project title** — short answer; required; 5–180 characters.
3. **Primary department/campus** — canonical dropdown; required.
4. **Public project description** — paragraph; required; include problem,
   approach, student role, and expected outcome.
5. **Should the listing open immediately at launch?** — Open/Keep closed;
   required.
6. **Total student seats** — number; required; 1–50.
7. **Expected project duration** — short answer; optional.
8. **Expected weekly student commitment** — number of hours; optional.

### Section 2: eligibility and work plan

9. **Who may apply?** — checkboxes: B.Tech, M.Tech, Ph.D., specific year(s),
   interdisciplinary applicants, other.
10. **Prerequisite skills or courses** — paragraph; required or `None`.
11. **Preferred skills** — paragraph; optional.
12. **Student responsibilities** — paragraph; required.
13. **Planned milestones or timeline** — paragraph; required.
14. **Expected deliverables** — paragraph; required.
15. **Selection criteria** — paragraph; required; do not request protected or
    irrelevant personal attributes.
16. **Safety, ethics, privacy, laboratory, or approval requirements** — paragraph;
    required or `None`.

### Section 3: files and application questionnaire

17. **Project brief/PDF/material link** — URL, document reference, descriptive
    text, or `NA`; required. The faculty owner can update this later.
18. **Does this project require a separate application questionnaire?** — Yes/No;
    required.
19. **Published Google Form URL** — URL; required only when Question 18 is Yes.
20. **If no form exists, should the launch team provide the approved questionnaire
    template?** — Yes/No; shown when Question 18 is No.
21. **Application closing date or review cadence** — date/short answer; optional;
    currently informational until deadline support is implemented.
22. **Who will review applications?** — checkboxes: Project owner, named faculty
    co-reviewer, departmental committee; required. Current pilot authorization
    permits the project owner only, so other choices require a future approved
    workflow.

### Section 4: collaboration and publication readiness

23. **Will you accept non-seat student contribution proposals after seats are
    filled?** — Yes/No; required. The pilot currently exposes this workflow by
    default, so a No answer would require a product change.
24. **Are faculty collaboration requests welcome for this project?** — Yes/No;
    required.
25. **Preferred faculty collaboration types** — checkboxes; conditional.
26. **Can this project be publicly displayed before application opening?** —
    Yes/No; required.
27. **Is any project information confidential, embargoed, export-controlled, or
    awaiting IP review?** — Yes/No plus explanation; required.
28. **Final confirmation: the project description, linked material, capacity, and
    questionnaire are accurate and approved for the stated visibility.** —
    checkbox; required.

## Decisions required before form creation

1. Name the institutional owner and response-review team.
2. Approve which fields are public, restricted, or optional.
3. Decide whether employee reference/evidence is collected in this form or in a
   separate restricted verification process.
4. Approve the canonical designation and employment-type choices.
5. Decide whether launch accepts projects without PDFs or Google Forms.
6. Decide whether faculty may opt out of non-seat contribution and peer
   collaboration workflows.
7. Define response retention, correction, withdrawal, and deletion procedures.
8. Approve the privacy notice and institutional-branding language.
